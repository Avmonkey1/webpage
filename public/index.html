Getting blank pages or login errors?

LinkedIn requires login for most profiles. To fix this, configure your LinkedIn session cookie on the server:

  1. Log into LinkedIn in Chrome
  2. Press F12 → Application tab → Cookies → https://www.linkedin.com
  3. Find the cookie named li_at and copy its value
  4. On your server, add this to your .env file:
    LINKEDIN_LI_AT_COOKIE=paste-value-here
  5. Restart the server: pm2 restart linkedin-pdf

When configured, the status badge above will show "Authenticated mode".
